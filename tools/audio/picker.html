How this works

Every slot the game can play a sound into is on the left. Pick one, audition candidates in the middle, assign the one that fits, tune it on the right. When the required slots are green, hit Copy configuration.

Picks are kept in this browser's local storage, so a reload does not lose them. Nothing is uploaded; the audio streams straight from the source.
