CHANGES MADE
------------

The SRD 5.1 text has been reformatted into machine-readable JSON for use by a
rules engine. Descriptive prose has been abridged, and machine-resolvable
`effect`, `traits[].id`, and `actions[].extra` records have been added by the
authors of this project to let code adjudicate the rules. Content selection is
a Phase 1 subset (four classes at levels 1-5, four races, ~49 spells of levels
0-3, ~29 monsters of CR 0-5, the full weapon and armor tables, and the fifteen
conditions plus exhaustion). Any errors introduced by that transformation are
the responsibility of this project, not of Wizards of the Coast LLC.
